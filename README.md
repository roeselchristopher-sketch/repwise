# Repwise

A personal training app for iPhone: a weekly gym plan built from your goal (burn fat, build muscle or recomp), a workout logger with progressive overload, weekly check-ins that adjust calories and volume, progress charts, and a food tracker with barcode scanning.

**Open it:** https://roeselchristopher-sketch.github.io/repwise/

On iPhone, open the link in Safari and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It then runs full-screen like an app and also works offline.

## Where your data lives

Everything you log stays in your browser on that device. Nothing is sent to this repository or any server. Use **Me → Copy backup** now and then, and **Restore** to move data to another device.

## Features

- Onboarding questionnaire → personalised split (full body, upper/lower, push/pull/legs) for gym machines, free weights, home dumbbells or bodyweight
- Workout logger: previous performance per set, suggested weights (double progression), rest timer, exercise swaps, 6-week blocks with a deload week
- Weekly check-in: calorie and volume adjustments from your weight trend
- Progress: calendar, streaks, body weight, estimated 1-rep max, sets per muscle
- Food: calorie and macro targets, common Dutch foods, live barcode scanning with [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org) lookup
- Apple Watch: import steps, active energy, exercise minutes and weight via an iPhone Shortcut (setup guide in the app)

Product data comes from Open Food Facts, available under the Open Database License. Repwise gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice.
